The Problem

Debugging production web apps shouldn’t feel like guesswork

DevTools isn’t available to QA, support, or non‑technical users. RUM tools send data to dashboards, but don’t show what’s happening right now. Console logs vanish on refresh. Network issues and performance problems hide between tools.

The Solution

Observability that lives inside your app

InitLogs is a lightweight, zero‑dependency observability panel that runs directly in the browser. It captures everything happening inside your application and displays it in a clean, powerful UI that anyone can open.

A debugging superpower — built for real teams

How It Works

Drop it in. Open the panel. See everything.

InitLogs is intentionally simple to adopt. You don’t need to refactor your app, set up a backend, or learn a new framework.

1. Add the InitLogs script to your app

Include InitLogs via a script tag or bundler. It initializes itself quietly, instruments the browser, and waits in the background until you open it.

2. Open the panel with a hotkey

Press Ctrl + Shift + L. The InitLogs panel slides in and starts streaming events in real time — interactions, logs, errors, network, and more.

3. Inspect, export, or forward

Filter and inspect events in‑page, export them for debugging, or forward them to your own systems for storage and analysis.

Works where your users are
